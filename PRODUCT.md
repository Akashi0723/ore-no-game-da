# Wildbound

A Roblox creature exploration game with exclusive wild catches and sanctuary theft. Players earn coins through garden jobs and clue photography, buy snacks, sneak up on creatures, and build a collection. Taming XP unlocks tools; three distinct garden discoveries unlock the pond. Safe vault storage contrasts with exposed sanctuary displays. Raids require extraction and a 60-second chase; three net hits recover a stolen creature.

Audience: Roblox desktop and mobile players who like collecting, discovery, and competitive chases. First interaction should happen within 30 seconds. Existing GitHub/Rojo workflow must remain supported. No third-party asset dependencies. Real Robux raids remain disabled until configured and validated; Studio supports free raid testing.

The requested implementation authorizes routine design and coding decisions. No extra per-surface approval gates are imposed. Deliver a playable first version and clearly distinguish compiler/package validation from Studio runtime playtesting.

# Wildbound visual system

Direction: a warm naturalist field journal over a soft low-poly garden. Cream surfaces (#F6F2E8), forest ink (#183F32), muted leaf (#6F8D5C), berry (#B95068), gold (#D3A44C), slate (#51685D). Native Roblox fonts: FredokaOne for game title and creature names, GothamMedium/GothamBold for controls, Gotham for readable copy. Use consistent 8px spacing rhythm, 12px corners on panels, readable 15–18px body text, 48px primary touch controls, modest borders instead of glass.

The world remains visible. Desktop HUD: title/weather upper left, wallet/taming upper right, compact objective left, journal/shop/sanctuary navigation below, contextual action and sneak lower right away from Roblox jump control. On small screens shorten copy and use a horizontal compact header; panels occupy a safe inset viewport with internal scrolling. Menus use creature portraits and concrete discovery descriptions; dark forest buttons carry primary actions, cream controls secondary. Amber marks exposed ownership and raid risk; red is reserved for errors and active threat.

Motion: 160–220ms panel entrance, a short portrait reveal for successful befriending, subtle creature bobbing, no continuous HUD pulsing. Every control has visible selection, pending, disabled, and focus states. Gamepad selection and keyboard shortcuts should complement touch.
